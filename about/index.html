Profile

The work behind the notes.

I study binary security, mobile system components, and IoT and network devices. Starting with firmware, protocols, and runtime behavior, I trace data flows, privilege boundaries, and the root causes of vulnerabilities.

My work spans attack-surface modeling, vulnerability discovery, dynamic validation, impact assessment, and reporting. These notes document the analysis, experimental evidence, and questions that remain open.

Method

From a finding to evidence.

Model the boundary.

Record target versions, callers, and required privileges to define the attack surface and scope of validation.

Test the impact.

Combine static and dynamic analysis with ordinary-app attack paths and negative controls to test actual impact.

Keep the evidence.

Use research ledgers, candidate queues, and evidence inventories to distinguish static leads, dynamically confirmed behavior, and reportable findings.
